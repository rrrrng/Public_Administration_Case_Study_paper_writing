# Paper Quality Criteria (PA Case Study Research)

Use this checklist to decide whether a case study paper is worth close reading/citing, and to diagnose gaps in your own manuscript.

---

## 1) Contribution quality

- Is there a one-sentence contribution claim?
- Does it advance theory via mechanisms/propositions/boundaries (not only “interesting case”)?
- Is the theory dialogue explicit and coherent?

---

## 2) Mechanism quality (how/why)

- Are actors, constraints, strategies, interactions, and outcomes linked?
- Are alternatives/counter-evidence engaged?
- Are concepts used mechanistically (not as labels)?

---

## 3) Evidence chain quality (traceability)

- Can key claims be traced to evidence type and source/time/material IDs?
- Is triangulation present, or single-source dependence?
- Is an appendix/audit trail provided?

---

## 4) Methods transparency and credibility

- Is case selection logic defensible and aligned with the RQ?
- Are analysis steps described (coding/process tracing) with iteration and checks?
- Is ethics/anonymity handled?

---

## 5) Generalization and boundary conditions

- Are claims conditional (“when… then…”)?
- Are boundary conditions explicit and consistent with limitations?
- Is analytic generalization logic discussed (what transfers and why)?
