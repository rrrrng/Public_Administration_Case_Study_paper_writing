Demand Coupling and Scenario Adaptation: The Government’s Action Logic in the Digital Transformation
of Grassroots Public Services
Li Yang
［Abstract］ D igital transformation is a important way to improve the quality and efficiency of grassroots public
services. The government plays a central role in driving this transformation，and its actions significantly
influence the success of these efforts. Existing research often treats all levels of government below the
central government as a whole，overlooking the complexity of interactions between different governmental
tiers. By examining the interactive dynamics between upper and lower levels of government，a coupled
transformation analysis framework is developed. Three action paths are identified：upward authority
borrowing based on ambiguous policies，downward demand integration based on information power，and
scenario adjustments in the coupled transformation process. A case study of the“ One-Click Extension”
initiative in District A of Shanghai reveals that，leading sector strengthen its resource integration
and mobilization capabilities through strategies such as policy endorsement，authority leveraging，
and information discretion. These actions allow them to align the vague requirements of higher-level
authorities with the specific needs of grassroots communities，ensuring that transformation scenarios are
applicable and effective. The coupled transformation embodying the action orientation of experimentalist
governance and the public service logic of policy reproduction，value co-creation，and agile governance.
